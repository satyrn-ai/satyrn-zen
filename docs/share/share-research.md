# Share and find research papers on small open-source models

!!! info "About this guide"

    This guide provides a list of sites to find research papers on small open-source models.
    These sites are also useful for sharing preprints and research papers.

    Please open an issue or PR to add a site to this guide.

## Sites

- [Search arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/search):
  arXiv is a free distribution service
  and an open-access archive for nearly 2.4 million scholarly articles in the fields
  of physics, mathematics, computer science, quantitative biology, quantitative finance,
  statistics, electrical engineering and systems science, and economics.
  Materials on this site are not peer-reviewed by arXiv.

- [Papers by Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/huggingface/papers):
  Read papers authored by Hugging Face Papers.

- [Papers on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers):
  Search papers on AI/ML from Hugging Face community.

- [Search Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/):
  Google Scholar is a search engine for academic literature.
