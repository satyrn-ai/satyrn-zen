# A vision of the Satyrn ecosystem


## Satyrn's Core

Our initial focus

1. **Humans** who want trusted and transparent AI Models and tools
2. High quality learning materials that help the community learn, create, and share ideas and expertise
   - serve beginners and experts and everyone in between
   - be concrete in our language and terms; reduce jargon so it is not a barrier
3. Technical projects
   - A fully open, local model
   - Tools that work from terminal, web (notebooks), and as local applications
   - Tools that leverage Python's technical strengths


## Satyrn Inner Ring 1 - Open Python Tools and Libraries

Share knowledge about open source Python Tools and packages from across the ecosystem
that serve our mission.

## Satyrn Inner Ring 2 - Domain specific tools and libraries

Here our focus is on specific domains and how they use Satyrn's tools and philosophy
- Medical
- Community advocacy
- Climate research
- Education
- and more

## Satyrn in the Universe

We recognize that "one-size does not fit all".

The universe contains models and tools that are closed or not fully open, languages other than Python.
We try to clarify what other constellations (tools) in the universe offer, their benefits, their limitations, and their use cases.

Python's 30+ years of success was consistent focus on the education and growth of the community. Education is a Python superpower
on par with the language.
